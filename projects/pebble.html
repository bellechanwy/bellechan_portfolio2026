Project 04 · E-commerce · Checkout

A checkout that respects the planet.

Pebble is a sustainable-goods marketplace. We rebuilt the cart and checkout around carbon transparency — and bumped conversion by 19% without resorting to dark patterns.

Role
Service + Product Designer
Team
1 designer, 1 researcher, 3 engineers
Year
2022 → 2023
Scope
Service · UX · A11y
The problem

Shoppers cared about sustainability — until checkout.

Pebble's brand promise was "every purchase, fully traceable." But cart abandonment was 74% and post-purchase NPS cited "I had no idea what I was paying for at the end."

The checkout was a Shopify default with a sustainability label painted over it. The carbon impact, the offset costs, the shipping origin — all tucked away inside collapsed accordions.

Constraints

  • WCAG 2.2 AA compliance — non-negotiable, audited externally.
  • Must support split shipments from 4 fulfilment regions.
  • Must surface carbon data without making the page heavier.
Approach

We made the carbon cost the first number, not a footnote.

  • Move 01

    Cart-as-receipt

    Each item shows price, weight, and grams of CO₂e — surfaced on the cart line itself, not three taps deep.

  • Move 02

    Honest shipping

    A live map showing where your items ship from. "Fastest" is renamed "soonest"; the slow option says how much CO₂ you save.

  • Move 03

    Frictionless A11y

    Single-column layout, 44px tap targets, skip-to-pay shortcut, full keyboard parity. WCAG 2.2 AA from day one.

Outcomes · 6 months post-launch

Conversion went up because we were honest, not in spite of it.

+19% Cart-to-purchase conversion lift, holdout-validated.
+9% Average order value as users grouped shipments.
100% WCAG 2.2 AA pass rate at external audit, no exceptions.

Project 04 · E-commerce · Checkout

A checkout that respects the planet.

Pebble is a sustainable-goods marketplace. We rebuilt the cart and checkout around carbon transparency — and bumped conversion by 19% without resorting to dark patterns.

Role
Service + Product Designer
Team
1 designer, 1 researcher, 3 engineers
Year
2022 → 2023
Scope
Service · UX · Accessibility
My role

Solo designer, embedded for six months.

I was the only designer on this engagement, working alongside a UX researcher and three engineers. I owned the service design (journey mapping, touchpoint audit), the interaction design for cart and checkout, and the accessibility review against WCAG 2.2 AA.

I also liaised with the external auditor at the end of the project to address any accessibility findings before launch.

Approach

Make the carbon cost the first number, not a footnote.

Pebble's brand promise was "every purchase, fully traceable" — but the checkout was a Shopify default with a sustainability badge painted on. The carbon impact, offset cost, and shipping origin were all buried inside collapsed accordions. Shoppers cared about sustainability until they hit the cart — then the interface stopped supporting that value.

We moved carbon data onto the cart line itself — price, weight, and grams of CO₂e visible without a tap. The shipping selector was renamed: "Fastest" became "Soonest" and the slow option showed exactly how much CO₂ you'd save.

Outcome · 6 months post-launch

Conversion went up because we were honest, not in spite of it.

Cart-to-purchase conversion lift +19%
Average order value increase +9%
WCAG 2.2 AA pass rate 100%

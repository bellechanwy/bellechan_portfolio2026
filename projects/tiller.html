Project 02 · Web · SaaS dashboard

Finance for tiny teams.

Tiller is a no-jargon books app for two-person companies. We rebuilt cashflow, expenses and tax filings around a single principle: the person doing this work isn't an accountant.

Role
Senior Product Designer
Team
2 designers, 5 engineers, 1 PM
Year
2023 → 2024
Scope
UX · UI · Design system
My role

Redesign lead and design-system owner.

I joined as the senior designer on a two-person design team and owned the end-to-end redesign of the core dashboard — from initial audit through to handoff. I was also responsible for establishing the design system that underpinned every new screen going forward.

I worked closely with the PM to scope the quarter, ran weekly design crits with engineering, and presented direction to the CEO at each milestone.

Approach

Two front doors, one set of data.

The existing dashboard tried to serve accountants and founders with a single view — dense tables, 14 filters above the fold, accounting acronyms throughout. Support tickets confirmed it: "I don't know what this number means" was the top complaint.

Rather than strip features, I introduced two modes. Today view answered the three questions tiny-team founders actually ask. Ledger view kept everything power users relied on. Same data, different doors.

Outcome · 5 months post-launch

Fewer "what does this mean?" tickets. More renewals.

NPS lift, non-accountant accounts +34
Reduction in "what does this mean?" tickets −47%
Annual-plan upgrade rate +22%

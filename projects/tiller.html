Project 02 · Web · SaaS dashboard

Finance for tiny teams.

Tiller is a no-jargon books app for two-person companies. We rebuilt cashflow, expenses and tax filings around a single principle: the person doing this work isn't an accountant.

Role
Senior Product Designer
Team
2 designers, 5 engineers, 1 PM
Year
2023 → 2024
Scope
UX · UI · Design system
The problem

The dashboard had every feature an accountant could want — and scared everyone else.

Tiller's NPS was sitting at +8, and support tickets were dominated by one phrase: "I don't know what this number means." Users were a freelance designer, a small bakery owner, a two-person consultancy — none of them spoke balance-sheet.

Meanwhile, every screen was optimised for the 5% of users who did: dense tables, accounting acronyms, fourteen filters above the fold.

Constraints

  • Cannot remove existing accountant-grade features — pro users rely on them.
  • Must integrate with three existing tax APIs (UK, SG, AU) on day one.
  • One quarter to ship the redesigned core flow.
Approach

Two front doors, one set of data.

We didn't redesign the app — we redesigned the front door. Power users got "Ledger view" (everything visible, dense, fast). Everyone else got "Today view" — a single column of plain-English cards that answered the three questions tiny teams actually have.

  • Question 01 Can I pay my bills this month?
  • Question 02 Is this expense a tax thing?
  • Question 03 What do I owe, and to whom?
Design system

We shipped the redesign and a 200-token system in the same quarter.

  • Tokens

    Three-tier semantic palette

    Raw → semantic → component. Lets us flip light/dark and brand without rewriting screens.

  • Components

    38 audited primitives

    Replaced 142 one-off variants with 38 composable primitives. Engineers loved us for one whole sprint.

  • Docs

    Anatomy + do / don't

    Every component shipped with a one-page "anatomy + when not to use it" Figma doc. No more PRs to "explain the variant."

Outcomes · 5 months post-launch

Fewer "what does this mean?" tickets. More renewals.

+34 NPS lift among non-accountant accounts.
−47% Reduction in "What does this number mean?" support tickets.
+22% Annual-plan upgrade rate from monthly subscribers.

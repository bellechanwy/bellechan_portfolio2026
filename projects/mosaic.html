Project 03 · Web · Discovery flow

Music discovery for the slow listener.

We swapped a streaming service's endless infinite-scroll feed for a small set of human-curated weekly mixtapes — and watched the most engaged segment of users grow.

Role
UX Strategy + Visual Design
Team
3 designers, 1 motion, 4 engineers
Year
2023
Scope
Strategy · UI · Motion
My role

UX strategy and visual design lead.

I led UX strategy and visual design within a three-person design team. My focus was on the new discovery model — defining the information architecture, the editorial concept, and the visual language for the mixtape rooms.

I collaborated with the motion designer on the entrance animations and worked directly with engineering during the React component build to ensure the transitions matched the spec.

Approach

Fewer choices. More taste.

The discovery feed had accumulated 80+ algorithmic rails — moods, charts, made-for-you, podcasts — each competing for the same space. Qualitative research kept surfacing the same line: "I don't know what to listen to."

We replaced the wall of rails with three named rooms — Late, Loud, Lush — curated weekly by an in-house editorial team and introduced with a hand-written editor's note. The reduction in choice was the feature.

Outcome · 90 days post-launch

We optimised for taste — and the metrics caught up.

Average session length on Discover +28%
Save-to-library rate per session +12%
Skip rate in curated mixtape rooms −18%

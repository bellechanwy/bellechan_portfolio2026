Project 03 · Web · Internal product

Please submit your timesheet.

Rethinking the timesheet submission experience by moving from a third-party platform to an in-house product designed around the real workflows of both employees and the RMO team. The platform is now live and actively used by over 400 users weekly.

Role
Sole Product Designer
Team
RMO stakeholders, PM, engineering
Year
2023
Scope
UX · UI · Workflow design
My role

Designing a system people wouldn’t dread using every week.

As the sole designer on the project, I worked closely with the RMO team to untangle messy workflows, repetitive tasks, and everyday frustrations hidden behind timesheet submissions and allocations. I gathered requirements, mapped pain points, and looked for ways the system could work smarter for the people managing it behind the scenes.

At the same time, I spoke to employees across the ground to understand where the process felt confusing, tedious, or unnecessarily difficult to navigate. From there, I designed a simpler experience that made submitting timesheets feel faster, clearer, and far less painful for everyone involved.

Approach

Making an unavoidable process feel less like a chore.

The approach focused on listening closely to both sides of the process. Through workflow mapping, stakeholder interviews, and iterative UX thinking, we identified friction points and simplified key interactions across submission, review, and approval flows. The result was a more intuitive in-house platform that balanced business efficiency with everyday usability.

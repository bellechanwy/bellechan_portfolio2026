Project 01 · Desktop · Trading platform

Bringing transparency to carbon trading.

Where geospatial intelligence meets AI to transform natural ecosystems into trusted carbon assets. Climate accountability, transparency, and sustainable stewardship is now tangible at a national scale.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Team
2 Designers, 8 Engineers, 1 PM
Year
2023 → 2024
Scope
Research · UX/UI · Strategy
My role

Designing trust across ecosystems: concept to future-state vision.

I worked across systems thinking and experience design to shape how sovereign carbon assets could be created, monitored, and understood. From stakeholder workflows and platform concepts to proposal storytelling and AI-enabled experiences, I translated complex climate and geospatial systems into something people could navigate, trust, and act on.

I also helped ideate on future-facing interactions around emissions intelligence, digital twins, and real-time environmental monitoring — balancing technical ambition with clarity, governance, and usability.

Approach

Turning invisible ecosystems into visible, trusted systems.

The challenge wasn’t just measuring carbon. It was building confidence across governments, industries, and ecosystems with different incentives, data sources, and levels of trust.

The design team’s approach focused on making complex environmental intelligence feel legible and actionable. Using design thinking, we mapped ecosystem actors, surfaced governance and transparency needs, and explored AI-driven concepts that could make carbon monitoring more real-time, interactive, and accountable at national scale.

Project 01 · Mobile · iOS + Android

A softer way to fall asleep.

Lumen is a bedtime companion that nudges you off your phone — wind-down rituals, gentle sound mixes, and a sleep journal that doesn't shame you for an off night.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Team
Solo design + 2 engineers
Year
2024 → 2025
Scope
Research · UX · UI · Brand
My role

End-to-end design — from brief to build.

I was the sole designer on Lumen, embedded with a two-person engineering team. I led every design phase: stakeholder alignment, user research, information architecture, visual design, and handoff — from kickoff to App Store submission.

I also set up the component library in Figma and wrote the design documentation that the engineers used to build without back-and-forth.

Approach

One tap, three quiet rooms, no menu fatigue.

Research surfaced a consistent pattern: people who used meditation apps still reached for social media the moment the session ended. The design problem wasn't onboarding or content — it was the gap between "session over" and "phone face-down."

The approach was to remove choice at night. Instead of a feature menu, Lumen presents a single gentle nudge — a 6-minute wind-down ritual that ends with the screen dimmed and audio fading out. No next step to tap.

Outcome · 6 months post-launch

Less time in the app. More time asleep.

Average pre-sleep phone time −31%

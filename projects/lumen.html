Project 01 · Mobile · iOS + Android

A softer way to fall asleep.

Lumen is a bedtime companion that nudges you off your phone — wind-down rituals, gentle sound mixes, and a sleep journal that doesn't shame you for an off night.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Team
Solo design + 2 engineers
Year
2024 → 2025
Scope
Research · UX · UI · Brand
Overview

The brief was simple. The behaviour change wasn't.

Lumen launched as a meditation app, but the data told a different story: 72% of nightly sessions were started after 11pm, and most users dropped off the moment a session ended — straight back into TikTok.

We rebriefed the product around a sharper problem: help people actually put their phone down. Not "use the app more" — use it less, but at the right moment.

Goals

  • Reduce average pre-sleep screen time by 25%.
  • Make the app feel inviting at night without being clinical.
  • Build a sleep journal that reads like a friend, not a lab report.
Research

18 sleep-deprived strangers told us what was actually broken.

We ran 18 contextual interviews in people's bedrooms — phones in hand, lights low. We mapped the last 30 minutes before sleep for each participant and tagged every "phone reach."

Three patterns emerged:

  • The handoff. Most people closed a meditation, then immediately opened a social app. The bridge between "winding down" and "asleep" was missing.
  • The shame loop. Tracking apps that scolded users for poor sleep made the next night worse, not better.
  • The audio detour. People liked sounds, but disliked picking from menus at night. They wanted something close to "press play and dim everything."
Solution

One tap, three quiet rooms, no menu fatigue.

  • Feature 01

    Wind-down ritual

    A 6-minute guided sequence — breathing, light dimming, a written intention. The same shape every night, by design.

  • Feature 02

    Soft sound rooms

    Three pre-mixed sound rooms (Rain, Cabin, Library). No menu. Big tappable cards that invite sleepy fingers.

  • Feature 03

    Kind journal

    Three sliders, one prompt. We ditched the data-dense graphs and replaced them with weekly "letters to yourself."

Outcomes · 6 months post-launch

Less time in the app. More time asleep.

−31% Average pre-sleep phone time among active users.
+44 NPS lift over the previous version of the app.
4.8★ App Store rating after relaunch (up from 3.6).

My role

Connecting user needs with product and delivery decisions.

I worked across product analysis, UX evaluation, and delivery coordination to assess how the platform supported day-to-day records management workflows. Through audits, stakeholder discussions, and feature analysis, I identified usability gaps and improvement opportunities across both user experience and system functionality.

I also supported project-level coordination to help align product recommendations, delivery priorities, and implementation timelines across teams.

Approach

Simplifying workflows in systems built around complexity.

The challenge wasn’t just improving features. It was making enterprise records management feel more efficient and easier to navigate without disrupting operational requirements.

The approach focused on understanding how users interacted with the platform in real-world scenarios. By evaluating workflows, friction points, and system behaviours against industry best practices, I developed recommendations that balanced usability, governance, and delivery feasibility.

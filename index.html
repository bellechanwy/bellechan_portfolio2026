About

How I work

A small, opinionated process — sized to the problem, not the calendar.

  1. Step 01

    Listen first

    Stakeholder interviews, user calls, log dives. I leave with one sentence describing the real job-to-be-done.

  2. Step 02

    Sketch loudly

    Five flows on paper, three in low-fi, one in Figma. Cheap iterations, early opinions, async critique with the team.

  3. Step 03

    Ship, then sand

    Hand off with engineers in the room. Polish in production with real data — not in a perfect mock that never ships.
